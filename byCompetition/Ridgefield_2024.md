# Ridgefield 2024

### Question Set 1

1. Tell us some of the things your team is proud about this season - it could be some of the things your team has accomplished, what obstacles your team has overcome, or anything else you feel is important to your team.
2. Tell us how your team developed your gameplay and skills strategy.
3. What do you think is the most creative or unique aspect of your robot?
4. Tell us about your robot and how it has evolved throughout the season.
5. What was the biggest challenge you faced when building your robot?
6. Tell us about your programming and how it has evolved throughout the season.
7. How does your team track progress and ensure that tasks are being accomplished in a timely manner?
8. Is there anything else you would like to share with us regarding your team, your robot, or your game strategy?

### Question Set 2

1. Here is a list of the steps of the engineering design process. Can you explain each step, and for each one, provide an example from your season?
2. Can 3 people tell me what step of the engineering design process you think is the most fun? Feel free to respond as individuals, you don’t have to have a consensus.
3. How do you manage time and resources?
4. How do you collaborate and divide responsibilities?
5. Tell us about your gameplay and skills strategy.
6. Tell us how your team developed your overall robot design.
7. Tell us about your programming.
8. Is there anything else you would like to share with us regarding your team, your robot, or your game strategy?
